## What we hold

- **Your account** — the email address you signed up with, held by our
  authentication provider.
- **Your profile** — the name you choose, your skill level, home club, a short
  bio, and a photo if you upload one.
- **Your games** — the games you create or join, and who else is in them.
- **Your messages** — what you write in game and group chats.
- **Your results** — match scores, who played, who turned up, and the rating
  those produce.
- **Your connections** — the other players you have connected with.
- **Blocks and reports** — who you have blocked, and any report you have filed.

## Who can see it

Your name, photo, skill level and home club are visible to other signed-in
players. A game link shared into a group chat shows the venue, time, level and
how many spots are left to anyone who opens it — but **not** who is playing.

Blocks are private to you. A player is never told they have been blocked or
reported.

## What we do with it

Run the app: show you games near your level, let you join them, message the
other players and record results. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from your profile settings, in the app. It happens
immediately and cannot be undone.

**Deleted outright:** your login, profile, photo, chat messages, connections,
notifications, blocks, reports, and your place in any game or waiting list.

**Kept, with your name removed:** match results you played in, and games you
organised that other people joined. These are other people's records too — a
confirmed match is three other players' history, and deleting it would rewrite
their stats. Once your profile is gone, those records no longer name you.

Games you organised are handed to another player so they can still go ahead.

## Who else processes it

Two services outside Padel Loop, both hosted in the EU.

**PostHog** (product analytics) — EU cloud, Frankfurt, Germany. It records
which steps of the app you reach: signing up, finishing setup, opening a game,
joining one, entering and confirming a result, and opening a shared link. Each
record holds your account ID and things like a game ID, a count, or a yes/no.
It does **not** receive your name, email address, the clubs or venues you type,
or anything you write in chat. Your IP address is not collected, screen
recording is off, and automatic click-tracking is off.

**Sentry** (error monitoring) — EU region, Germany. When something breaks it
records the error, where in the code it happened, and the page address with any
query string removed. Request bodies, headers and cookies are stripped before
anything is sent, so login tokens never reach it. If you were signed in it
records your account ID and nothing else about you.

Neither is used for advertising, and neither receives anything you have typed.

## How long we keep it

While your account exists. After deletion, only the anonymised records above,
for as long as they matter to the other people in them.

## Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, and delete your account there
too. For anything else, get in touch.
