## 1. Who we are

Padel Loop helps players in Dublin find and join a padel game near them, at
their level.

## 2. Your account

You need an account to join a game. You are responsible for what happens under
your account, and for keeping your login details to yourself. You must be old
enough to enter into an agreement where you live.

## 3. Playing games

Games are arranged between players. Padel Loop lists them and helps you find
each other — it does not run the games, book the courts, take payment, or
supervise anything that happens on court. Turning up, paying the venue and
playing safely are between you, the other players and the club.

## 4. Behaving decently

Be someone other people want to play with. Do not harass, threaten or abuse
anyone, do not misrepresent who you are, and do not use Padel Loop to sell
things or run scams. You can report a player from their profile or from a chat
message, and you can block anyone you would rather not see.

## 5. Your content

Anything you write — your profile, chat messages, match scores — stays yours.
By posting it you allow us to show it to the other people it is meant for.

## 6. Ending it

You can delete your account at any time from your profile settings. Some shared
records survive, with your name removed — see the Privacy Policy for exactly
what and why. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms.

## 7. No promises about the service

Padel Loop is provided as it is. We do not guarantee it will always be
available, that a game will go ahead, or that anyone will turn up.

## 8. Changes

If these terms change materially we will say so in the app. Continuing to use
Padel Loop after that means you accept the new version.
