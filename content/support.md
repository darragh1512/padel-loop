# Support

Stuck, or something not working? Here’s how to sort the most common things.
You don’t need an account to read this page.

## Contact us

Email us at **[CONTACT EMAIL — TO ADD]** and we’ll get back to you as soon as
we can. It helps to tell us the email address you signed up with and, if
something went wrong, what you were doing when it happened.

## Delete your account

1. Open **Settings**: tap **Profile** on the bottom bar, then the gear at the top.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
3. Read what will be removed, type **DELETE**, then tap **Delete My Account**.

This can’t be undone. Your login, name, photo, profile, chat messages,
connections and notifications are deleted for good, and you leave any game or
waiting list you were in. Match results you played in stay, because they’re the
other players’ record too, but your name is removed from them. A game you
organised that others joined is handed to another player so it still goes
ahead.

## Report or block a player

**On their profile:** open the player’s profile, tap **•••** at the top right,
then choose **Report** or **Block**.

**In a chat:** tap **Report** next to the message. You can block the player at
the same time by turning on **Block as Well**.

When you block someone, their chat messages are hidden from you, they stop
showing up when you search for players, and any connection between you is
removed. In a game you’re both in, they show as “Blocked Player”.

Players are never told that you’ve blocked or reported them. Every report goes
to the Padel Loop team to look at. To unblock someone, go to **Settings → Blocked
Players** and tap **Unblock**.

## Reset your password

1. On the **Log In** screen, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the email address you signed up with and tap **Send Link**. If you’re already
   logged in, log out from **Settings** first.
3. Open the email from Padel Loop and tap the link in it.
4. Choose a new password. You’ll be logged in with it straight away.

No email after a few minutes? Check your spam or junk folder, then try again.
If it still doesn’t arrive, contact us at the address above.
